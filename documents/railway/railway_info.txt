Railway Station Information

Bhopal Junction is a major railway station in Madhya Pradesh.

Bhopal Junction provides facilities such as ticket counters, waiting rooms, restrooms, food facilities, parking and railway platforms.

Passengers can contact railway staff or the station master for assistance.

Rani Kamalapati railway station is another important railway station in Madhya Pradesh.

Railway stations may provide facilities including drinking water, toilets, waiting areas, food courts, parking, accessibility facilities and passenger assistance.

Passengers should check the railway station display boards for train arrival and departure information.
